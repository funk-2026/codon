# Codon — Teacher guide

Teachers create the content students practise with. Everything you make goes **Draft → In review → Approved → Live**: an admin reviews it, then **you** press **Publish**.

## Home
- **Quick actions:** New test, New video/document, New Brain Hack, **Question Bank**, **Reports**, **Corrections**.
- **Chapters that need questions:** chapters whose question pool for students' custom tests is thin, with how many more each needs (+N) and how many are yours.
- **Recent activity** — tap to open.

## Upload tab (create anything)
New Test · New Video / Document · New Brain Hack · Flashcard Decks. Uploading files is covered in **upload-manual.md**.

## Tests and questions
1. **New Test:** title, type (Q Bank / Test Series / Practice), location (course → subject → chapter), duration, marks per correct / wrong.
2. **Add questions** one by one, or **Bulk Upload via CSV / Import CSV** (+ a ZIP of images) — see the upload manual.
3. **Question editor**
   - Toolbar: **bold**, *italic*, sub (H~2~O), sup (x^2^), formula `$…$`, list, **image** (gallery or camera).
   - Images are shrunk automatically; add **alt text** (what the image shows) when asked.
   - **Student view** toggle shows exactly what students will see. **?** opens a formatting cheat-sheet.
   - Tag every question: subject, chapter, **difficulty**, topic, tags, NCERT class/page, source (PYQ + year…), "use in custom tests".
   - A **duplicate warning** appears if a similar question exists (it doesn't block saving).
   - Your typing is saved on the phone; if the app closes, reopen the same question → **Restore**.
4. **Submit for review.** If something's missing (e.g. difficulty), the list highlights exactly which questions and what.
5. After approval → **Publish**. Students see it immediately.

**Preview (My Content → a test):** see all questions, **Edit test & questions** (draft/rejected), **Edit question** on any card, and **Try it as a student** — a full dry run with timer, Check answer, explanation and the final score. Nothing is saved.

**Changing a live question:** tags/difficulty save instantly. Changing the wording, options or answer creates a **correction** that an admin approves; students keep the old version until then (track them under **Corrections**). A live question also shows **How students do**: answers, % correct, average time, whether results look easier/harder than its tag, and open reports.

## Question Bank
- All your questions across tests: search, quick filters (Reported, No difficulty, No chapter, No topic, No tags, Image without description), difficulty filter.
- **Long-press** to select many → bulk edit difficulty, chapter, tags, custom-test use, or move.
- **Fix chapter by chapter → Completeness:** per chapter, how many questions miss difficulty / tags / topic / NCERT / image alt text. Tap a number → **Quick-tag**: one question at a time — tap Easy/Medium/Hard (saves and moves on), pick or type tags, or **Open in editor** for topic/NCERT/alt text. Swipe left = skip, right = back.

## Reports inbox
Students report wrong answers, typos, image problems. **Open question**, fix it, then **Mark fixed** — or **No change needed** / **Dismiss** — with a note to the student. They're notified and see it under *My reports*. Questions with several reports are held for review automatically.

## Videos and documents
**New Video / Document:** title, location in the course, optional topic/notes → pick a video (uploads with progress, can retry) or write the document → **Preview & Submit**. Videos are processed after upload; students see "processing" until ready.

**Lecture PDFs (videos):** attach a **Notes PDF** (students download it any time) and an **Annotated PDF** (what you wrote while teaching — unlocks for each student after they watch ~90 % of the lecture). Attach them while creating the video or later from the video's preview → *Lecture PDFs* → Attach / Replace / Remove; changes reach students immediately, even on a live lecture.

## Brain Hacks
Short practical tips (title, category, text with images). **Save draft** or **Submit for review**. In **My Content → Brain Hacks** open one to **Edit** (draft / needs changes), **Resubmit**, **Publish** once approved, or **Delete**. Reviewer feedback shows at the top when changes are needed.

## Flashcard decks
**Upload → Flashcard Decks → +**
1. Details: title, description, course, optional subject/chapter, **Free for everyone** (off = subscribers only). **Create deck**.
2. **+ Add card:** front (prompt) and back (answer), each with formatting and one image. Edit, delete, move cards up/down.
3. **Submit for review** → after approval **Publish**. Editing locks while in review or live.

## My Content
Every test, video/document and Brain Hack with its status badge and ★ rating once students rate it. Filter by type, search, open to preview / edit / publish. Teachers with **platform-wide access** (granted by an admin) see everyone's content — a banner says so.
