# Codon — Student guide

Everything a student can do, screen by screen. Some features only appear when an admin has switched them on (marked **[flag]**).

## Getting in
- **Sign in:** enter your phone number → type the 6-digit code (or tap the code your phone suggests — it fills all boxes at once).
- **First time:** add your name and pick your course. You can change both later in Profile.

## Home
- **Greeting + day streak** (🔥 days in a row you practised).
- **Daily goal ring:** questions answered today vs. your goal. Tap it to set 10 / 20 / 30 / 50. Turns green when reached.
- **Updates carousel** (only when admins post something), **Continue** your last video/document, **Brain Hacks** shelf.
- **Explore tiles:** Q Bank, Test Series, Custom Test **[flag]**, Flashcards, Video Classes, Support. **Discover more →** opens Explore.
- **Explore:** Continue an in-progress test, Recommended for you, Quick start presets, Practise a chapter, Flashcards (top rated + new), Brain Hacks. Empty sections hide themselves.

## Practice tab
- **Q Bank / Test Series / Practice:** browse Subject → Chapter → tests. In a chapter, switch **Newest / Top rated** (★ rating shows once a test has 5+ ratings). 🔒 = needs a subscription.
- **Custom Test** card **[flag]** → the builder (see below).
- **Test history:** finished tests open the result; in-progress ones resume.

## Taking a test
- **Answer:** tap an option. **Clear my response** removes it.
- **Strike-through:** **long-press** an option to cross it out (it fades); long-press again to bring it back. Remembered if you leave and resume.
- **How sure are you?** After answering, optionally tap **Sure / Unsure / Guess** (tap again to clear). Used in your result analysis.
- **Mark for review** (flag icon), **Bookmark** (see Bookmarks), **Report** a problem with the question.
- **Palette** (grid icon): every question's state — answered, marked, both, current; **Next unanswered** jumps ahead.
- **Timer:** timed tests count down to the real deadline (at 0 the test submits itself). Untimed tests — e.g. Q Bank — show a **stopwatch** of time taken for the whole test. Leaving the app doesn't pause either.
- **Offline:** keep answering — a banner says how many answers are waiting to save; they upload when you're back online (even if the app was closed).
- **Tutor mode** (custom tests) **[flag]**: **Check answer** shows right/wrong and the explanation; the answer then locks.
- **Images:** tap a question image to zoom (pinch / double-tap, swipe down to close). Option images open full screen only in Review.
- **Formulas** (e.g. ½mv², chemical equations) are typeset; images of later questions load in the background.
- **Other device:** if the test is open on another phone you'll be asked to **Continue here**.
- Screenshots are blocked during tests.

## Result
- Score ring, correct / wrong / skipped, focus area, breakdown by subject, chapter and difficulty.
- **Time analysis:** average per question, total time, your 3 slowest questions (tap one → opens it in Review), time by subject, and hints when you rushed or got stuck.
- **Confidence vs. correctness:** how often you were right when Sure / Unsure / Guessing, and which "sure" answers were wrong.
- **Review Answers**, **Practise my mistakes** **[flag]** (wrong / skipped / both → a new test), **Retake**, **Rate the test** (stars), **Share result** (an image card with your first name, score, accuracy and streak).

## Review
- **2 minutes per question:** a countdown (top right) gives each question at most 2 minutes, then moves to the next one. Tap the timer to switch the limit off or on (remembered).
- Filters: All, Correct, Incorrect, Unattempted, Marked, Bookmarked.
- Per question: your answer vs. correct, explanation (👍/👎 "was this helpful?"), how many students got it right, your time and confidence, **personal note**, bookmark, report.

## Custom Test builder **[flag]**
1. **Start from:** **Repeat last** (your previous setup), admin presets (Daily 20, Weak areas…), or **My templates**. **Long-press a chip to build the test immediately.**
2. **Scope:** pick subjects / chapters / topics (or leave empty for everything).
3. **Difficulty:** easy / medium / hard filters; **mix** presets or **Custom** percentages (must total 100 %).
4. **Which questions:** unattempted, incorrect, bookmarked… **[flag: status filters]**; sources (PYQ, Q Bank…).
5. **Count, timing, mode** (Exam / Tutor), **marking** (NEET +4/−1, no negative, custom).
6. **Advanced:** question selection strategy, order, **subject balance** (Automatic / Even / Proportional / **Custom** weights), name.
7. The line at the bottom shows how many questions match live; if too few, tap a suggestion. **Generate**.
- **⋯ menu:** Save as template, **Share this setup** (link + code), Enter a share code, My saved templates, My custom tests, Reset.
- **Free plan:** limited questions per test and tests per day (shown in a banner).
- **My custom tests:** filter by state, resume, **New set** (same settings, fresh questions), View last result, Rename, Delete (scores are kept). **Templates** tab: tap to use, Share, Delete.

## Learn tab
- **Videos:** play, speed, quality, full screen, **mini player** (picture-in-picture button, top right — back arrow just goes back).
- **Video notes:** **+ Note at mm:ss** pauses and saves a note at that moment; tap a note's time to jump there; edit / delete. Bold, H~2~O, x^2^ and lists work.
- **Lecture PDFs** (under a video): **Notes** — download any time. **Annotated notes** (what the teacher wrote in class) — locked until you've watched about 90 % of the lecture, then downloadable. Download opens the share sheet (save to Files, open in a PDF app).
- **Documents:** read in the app.
- **Flashcards:** decks by subject; **Quick** mode (10 due cards across decks). Flip the card, then rate **Again / Hard / Good / Easy** — the app schedules it again accordingly (spaced repetition). Locked decks need a subscription.

## Progress (Profile → Your progress)
- Recommendations, score trend, coverage of the syllabus, **weak areas** with "Practise" buttons.
- **Mastery heat-map:** one tile per chapter — red needs work, amber improving, green strong, grey not enough data. Tap a tile → details + **Practise this chapter**.
- Streak and last 7 days.

## Bookmarks
- **Tap** the bookmark icon → saved to the list you used last. **Long-press** it → choose **Revise later / Doubts / Important**, move it, or remove it.
- **Profile → Bookmarks:** browse by list, move, remove, **Practise my bookmarks** **[flag]**.

## Support tab
- Articles in **Guidance / Motivation / Reflection Prompts**, a featured "Today" piece, About.

## Profile & settings
- Edit profile, **Subscription plans** → pay with Razorpay, **KYC** (only if required), **Manage devices**, **My reports** (status of questions you reported), **Notifications** inbox, Give feedback.
- **Settings:** theme, push notifications (asked only when you turn it on), streak reminders, report updates, **Daily practice reminder** (pick 7 AM / 5 / 7 / 9 PM — tapping it opens Practice).
